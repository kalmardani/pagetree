Bike Shed Rules

The shed behind building B holds 40 bikes. Spaces are not assigned.

Access

The door code changes on the first day of every quarter. Residents get the new code by e-mail from the building manager.

Storage

Bikes that have not moved for three months get a paper tag. Tagged bikes that are still there after 30 days are taken to the recycling centre.

Charging

E-bike batteries may be charged only at the two marked sockets & never overnight.
