# Office Coffee Machine Guide

## Cleaning

Descale the machine every four weeks with the citric acid tablets kept in the top drawer.
Empty the drip tray every evening.

## Ordering beans

Beans are ordered on the first Monday of the month. The office uses one 1 kg bag per week.
