# Riverside Tool Library Handbook

This handbook explains how the Riverside Tool Library works. The library lends hand tools,
power tools and garden equipment to members of the neighbourhood. It is run by volunteers
and funded by membership fees and small donations.

## Membership

### Who can join

Anyone aged 18 or over who lives, works or studies in the Riverside district can join.
Members under 18 may borrow hand tools only, and only together with a parent or guardian
who is also a member.

### Fees

The standard membership costs 30 euros per year. Students, pensioners and people receiving
unemployment benefits pay a reduced fee of 12 euros per year. Volunteers who give at least
four hours a month to the library pay no fee.

The fee is due on the anniversary of the day you joined. A membership that is more than
30 days overdue is paused until the fee is paid.

### Deposit

Every member pays a one time deposit of 20 euros. The deposit is returned in full when the
membership ends and all borrowed items are back.

## Borrowing

### How many items

A member can hold up to five items at the same time. Of these, at most two can be power
tools.

### Loan periods

Hand tools and garden equipment can be kept for 14 days. Power tools can be kept for 7 days,
because they are in higher demand. Ladders and the cement mixer can be kept for 3 days.

A loan can be extended once, by the same length, as long as nobody else has reserved the
item. Extensions are requested at the desk or by replying to the reminder email.

### Reservations

Members can reserve an item that is out on loan. When it comes back, the member is notified
and has 48 hours to collect it before the reservation lapses.

## Returns and late items

### Returning items

Items are returned to the desk during opening hours. Clean the item before returning it.
Garden tools must be free of soil, and paint brushes must be washed.

### Late returns

There is no money fine for a late return. Instead, a member who returns an item late cannot
borrow anything for the same number of days that the item was late. A member who is late
three times within twelve months loses the right to borrow power tools for six months.

### Lost or damaged items

Report damage when you return the item. Normal wear is not charged. If an item is lost or
damaged through misuse, the member pays the repair cost, up to the replacement value of the
item. The deposit can be used for this payment.

## Safety

### Training

Before a first loan of a chainsaw, hedge trimmer, angle grinder or circular saw, the member
must attend a free 45 minute safety session. Sessions run on the first Saturday of every
month at 10:00.

### Protective equipment

Safety glasses and hearing protection are lent free of charge with every power tool. Gloves
must be brought by the member.

## Opening hours and contact

The library is open on Tuesday and Thursday from 17:00 to 20:00, and on Saturday from 9:00
to 13:00. It is closed on public holidays and during the last two weeks of December.

Questions can be sent to the volunteer desk by email. Replies usually come within two working
days.
